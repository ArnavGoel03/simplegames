## Notes

- A: cause found. The board height budget is `100svh - --play-stage-chrome`
  (19.5rem), and the player rail and turn bar always stack above the board. On
  an iPad in landscape (about 740px of usable height) that leaves a board of
  roughly 428px on a 1180px wide screen. Fix: from the wide breakpoint, put the
  seats and turn bar beside the board and shrink the chrome budget to match.
  Ships only when the game release is unblocked (item 1); the five-site
  promotion fails closed on the pending migration.
- 4: alt text and any caption must reuse existing project strings (the game
  names). New wording goes to the owner, not into the site.
- 5: the approved privacy page says the studio site has no analytics, no
  beacon and no pixel. A client-side analytics script would make that false.
  Default chosen: read request counts per path from the Cloudflare Worker's own
  analytics (server side, nothing sent from the visitor's browser), so the
  privacy text stays true. A client script needs the owner to change that text
  first.
- B: the old layout checks only asked whether the board overflowed, never
  whether it was big enough, which is how a 382px board on a 1180px iPad passed.
  The device matrix asserts fit (whole play surface and its controls on screen,
  no sideways scroll, 44px taps) and fill (the play surface takes most of the
  screen along at least one axis) on every game screen at every listed device.
